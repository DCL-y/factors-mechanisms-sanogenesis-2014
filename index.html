Abstract

This collective monograph is the result of many years of collaboration between Ukrainian and foreign scientists. It introduces physicians of various specialties to modern instrumental and analytical tools for polysystemic sanogenetic monitoring and to the methodology of its use. Part I considers sanogenesis and adaptogenesis at the molecular and submolecular levels: epigenetic mechanisms controlling sanogenesis, epigenetic regulation of adaptive cellular immunogenesis in tumour transformation, and the magnetic field as an epigenetic factor. Part II addresses whole-organism regulation: automated systems for monitoring functional reserves, criteria for assessing sanogenetic status, and the instrumental base (spiroarteriocardiorhythmography, computerised assessment of psychomotor activity, laser correlation spectroscopy of biological fluids). Part III presents clinical applications: assessing the functional burden of essential hypertension and the adequacy of its treatment, evaluating the effectiveness of sanatorium rehabilitation, and assessing compensatory and adaptive reactions in people with disabilities, including spinal cord injury. The book is intended for medical researchers and practitioners and may be useful to medical students.
